Project Operational Pipeline

  1. Continuous Environmental Sensing: 8 sensor nodes sample smoke, heat, and optical flame readings.
  2. Autonomous Anomaly Classification: Threshold breaches trigger PRE-ALARM or FIRE states.
  3. Dynamic Risk Surface Propagation: Epicenter is blocked; surrounding corridors incur high risk penalties.
  4. Multi-Exit A* Pathfinding: Algorithm explores grid minimizing $f(n) = g(n) + h(n)$ where $g$ includes fire risk.
  5. Safety-First Optimization: Rejects shorter hazardous corridors in favor of safer alternative exits.
  6. Occupant Evacuation Guidance: Clear neon route guides occupants safely out of the building.
